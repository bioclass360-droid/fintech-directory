## 🏗️ Technical Architecture

Pionex aggregates liquidity from major global exchanges (like Binance and Huobi), meaning the "Order Management System" (OMS) is designed to find the best spread across multiple order books simultaneously.

### Built-in Automation Engines
Unlike traditional platforms that require external Webhooks, Pionex provides:
* **Grid Trading Engine:** High-frequency rebalancing within set price corridors.
* **Arbitrage Bot:** Automated monitoring of futures vs. spot price discrepancies.
* **DCA Bots:** Systematic accumulation protocols.

## 🛡️ Systems Reliability
Our audit focused on "Node Stability" during high-volatility events. Because the bots live "on-exchange," they are less susceptible to the API rate-limiting issues that plague retail traders using external tools.
