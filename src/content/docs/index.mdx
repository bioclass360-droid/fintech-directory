## What makes this directory different

We don't review trading platforms the way tech blogs review smartphones. Active systems traders — people running discretionary algos, scalping with DOM ladders, managing funded accounts — face a specific cognitive environment: high stakes, high speed, limited working memory.

Every platform we review is stress-tested against one question: **does this tool reduce your cognitive load at the moment it matters, or add to it?**

## The Attention Protocol

Our proprietary review framework evaluates every platform across three axes:

* **Execution Latency:** Order routing speed, API response time, and DOM refresh rates.
* **Visual Cognitive Load:** Information density, alert design, and decision hierarchy in the UI.
* **Psychological Friction:** How many clicks and confirmations stand between your decision and execution.

<Badge text="Methodology" variant="tip" /> Every ranking and score on this site maps back to these three core axes.
